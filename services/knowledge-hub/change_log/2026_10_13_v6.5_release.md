# v6.5 (2026-10-13) - from a local, single-user KH to a shared, signed-in service

v6.4 was the local-only KH: one person on one machine (`127.0.0.1:9000`), no sign-in, no notion of
users, local files (SQLite, Chroma, Kuzu). v6.5 is what now runs on SAP BTP for several people and
companies at once. Everything below is OFF by default, so a local install with no new settings still
behaves as v6.4 did. The dated files in this folder hold the detail of each step.

## Who may do what

- **SSO sign-in** (`app/auth.py`, `app/auth_rules.json`, 2026-09-25): Keycloak (HANA fork with the KH
  extension) signs people in through Google or Microsoft; KH checks the token on every request. Deny by
  default: a route without a rule is for maintainers only. Off while `KH_AUTH_ISSUER` is unset.
- **Roles and levels**: maintainer / member / tenant-admin; per project owner / editable / readonly.
  The rules live in tables of the identity service (`deploy/kh-auth` in the Keycloak repo), not in code.
- **Tenants** (2026-09-28): one group per company; tenant admins manage their own people; invite by
  e-mail; a live access catalog (`kh_catalog`).
- **Personal projects** (2026-09-30): each person's preferences and standing instructions are private
  to them (`personal-<email>`), created on first write. Since 2026-10-13 the store-worklog policy also
  puts logs that belong to no project the person may write there, so no log is lost.
- **Shared projects** (2026-10-13): semi-public (everyone reads) and public (everyone reads and edits)
  are decided by the identity service through roles carried by tenant groups; a tenant can be kept out.
- **Worklogs by author** (2026-09-26) and a hot log window per person.

## Running as a service

- **Every store on HANA** (2026-09-23/24): graph, vectors, relational tables and documents on SAP HANA
  Cloud, one switch `KH_DB_BACKEND=hana`; metadata filters run in the database (2026-09-30).
- **SAP BTP deploy** (`deploy/btp/push.ps1`); prod deploys from `main` (2026-10-12).
- **Local test stack** (`deploy/docker`): KH + the Keycloak fork, now on a local HANA express.
- **kh-helper 1.3 - 1.11**: per-CLI, per-profile sign-in; setup and removal; MCP server; companion MCP
  servers; hooks for Claude Code and other CLIs.

## Working together

- **Message stack** (2026-09-29): people and assistants leave messages for each other per project.
- **KH-to-KH sync** (`/home`, `/sync/*`): a maintainer pulls another KH's projects after an embedding
  handshake; rows are updated, never replaced; people come with their projects.
- **Turn context and step-wise context** (`/turn/context`, `/context/step`): one bounded pack per step
  instead of separate queries; entity cards over the worklogs; chunk vectors so long rows are searchable.
- **Distil** (2026-10-09/10): worklogs become small reviewed knowledge items (lessons, facts, procedures,
  habits, policy proposals), by hand or by the scheduled worker.
- **Policy action table** (from 2026-08-21): deterministic routing of every request to an action and its
  steps; a per-project override wins over the common rule.

## Upgrading from v6.4

- Nothing to do for a local install: with no `KH_AUTH_ISSUER` and no `KH_DB_BACKEND`, v6.5 keeps v6.4's
  behaviour and files.
- `emb_ver` on new rows reads `v6.5`; it is a stamp only, nothing compares it, so v6.4 rows need no
  re-embedding (same model, `bge-small-en-v1.5`).
- A service install needs the identity service first: see `docs/sign-in-and-access.md` and
  `change_log/2026_10_13_shared_projects_in_keycloak.md` for the deploy order.
